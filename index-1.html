Ideas need a plan

Learn how to fund what matters.

Money skills help turn a promising idea into a sustainable project. Start with understanding costs and testing demand before looking for outside funding.

Learning about investing is different from choosing investments. Financial markets involve risk; this section is educational, not personal financial advice.
01

Learn your money basics

Practise budgeting, saving, tracking expenses, and understanding the difference between revenue, cost, and profit.

02

Test before you spend

Talk to potential users, make a low-cost prototype, and check whether the problem is real before investing much time or money.

03

Explore youth-friendly support

Look into school clubs, supervised hackathons, innovation challenges, community grants, incubators, and mentor programmes. Check eligibility and rules.

04

Build a simple project pitch

Explain the problem, who it helps, your solution, estimated costs, expected impact, and what support you need.

From thought to action

Your next big thing starts with one small step.

You don't need a perfect plan to begin. You need curiosity, feedback, and a first experiment.

Spot a problem

Notice a frustration at school, home, online, or in your neighbourhood. Listen to the people affected.

Make it simple

Sketch a solution or build a tiny prototype with paper, free digital tools, or recycled materials.

Test & learn

Ask a few people to try it. Learn what works, what doesn't, and what should change.

Share your impact

Explain what you built, what you learned, and how others could help you improve it.
